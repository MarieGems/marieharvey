Bedroom Decor Finds: Lamps, Mirrors, and a Marble Vase

← Back to Random Thoughts
Marie Harvey · November 5, 2026

Bedroom decor finds that pull a room together usually come down to three things: one marble piece, one lighting fix, and one textile swap, not a full redecorate. The picks below come from my Household Decor list on Benable, which has 32 items between purple accents, marble pieces, lamps, mirrors, and wall art.

Disclosure: some links in this post are affiliate links, including my Benable link, and as an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you.

What is the easiest bedroom decor upgrade to start with?

A throw blanket, because it takes seconds to add and nothing to install. My Benable note on the Cozy Chunky Knit Throw Blanket says it is an easy way to layer texture onto a bed or reading chair without committing to a whole new comforter. It is on my Household Decor list, which also has a Purple Blankets & Throws Bedding category and a Bed Blankets category if you want more options at once.

Which marble piece is worth adding to a bedroom?

A vase, because it does the most with the least. My note on Marble Vases Home Decor says it is a simple way to add a finished, polished look to a shelf or console table without a full redecorating project. The same list links category pages for Marble Lamp, Marble Contact Paper Countertops, and Marble Coffee Tables, so compare the listings yourself if you want more than one marble piece in the room.

What is the easiest lighting upgrade for a bedroom?

A matching pair of lamps. My note on Lamp Sets says a matching pair is an easy way to make a bedroom feel finished without a full redesign, and buying them as a set keeps the sizing and light output consistent on both sides of the bed. The list also links Lamps Plus and Lamps For Living Room if you want to shop lighting more broadly, plus a Lamp Shades category for refreshing fixtures you already own.

How do you make a bedroom darker for actually sleeping in?

Blackout curtains. My note on Blackout Curtains For Bedroom says room-darkening curtains make a bigger difference in a bedroom than people expect, and that they are good for actually sleeping in past sunrise. The list also has a Purple Curtains & Drapes category if color matters as much as the blackout lining does.

What mirror and wall art fit a purple and cheetah bedroom?

Whatever carries the theme without matching it too literally. The list has a Moon Mirror and a Bedroom: Mirrors category, plus Purple Wall Art, Bedroom Wall Art from Wayfair, Art and Wall Decor, Cheetah Room Decor from Etsy, Dream Catchers, and Purple Cheetah Fabric from Spoonflower. None of these carry a written note from me, so they are named here as real options on the list, not individually reviewed pieces. Compare the listings for size and finish before you commit to one.
