Fall Decor for a House With Kids: Wipeable and Flameless

Fall decor for a house with kids has to survive sticky hands, a busy front door, and a table that gets used three times a day. What makes fall decor work in a house full of kids?

Four traits do most of the work: a surface you can wipe clean, no open flame, nothing that catches a door or a foot, and pieces you can swap in a couple of minutes. I am a mom of seven in Wichita, Kansas, so those four are the filter I apply to everything on my lists. A piece that needs careful handling is a poor fit for a busy house, however pretty it looks in a photo.

Choose each piece by how it fails: a placemat that wipes clean, a doormat that lies flat, a candle with no flame, and a pillow cover you can swap in two minutes.
