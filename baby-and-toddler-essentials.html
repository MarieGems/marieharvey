Which baby and toddler essentials are worth having?

The ones that solve a daily job, not the ones that fill a shelf. I am a mom of seven in Wichita, Kansas, so the lists lean toward gear that gets used over and over. The bottom line on baby and toddler essentials

Buy for the job that costs you the most time each day. If it is bottles, start with the warmer and washer. If it is mealtime, start with the plates and the hook-on chair.
