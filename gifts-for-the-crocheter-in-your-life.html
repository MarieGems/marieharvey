What yarn is on my list for a crocheter?

Bernat baby blanket yarn, in four separate colorways on my Business Supplies list: one, two, three, and four. None of the four carries a written note from me, so check each listing's exact color and skein size before you buy, since baby blanket yarn is bulkier than the 100% cotton yarn already named in gifts for book lovers.

Is Bernat baby blanket yarn the same as the cotton yarn in your other gift post?

No. Gifts for book lovers names the Katech 100% cotton yarn as a pairing for a crochet pattern book. Bernat baby blanket yarn is a different, bulkier yarn built for blankets specifically, which makes it a better fit for a bigger project than a small amigurumi piece.

What are safety eyes, and who are they a gift for?

Safety eyes are the small plastic eyes that snap into a crocheted or knitted toy, and they are a gift for someone making amigurumi specifically, not blanket or garment crochet. The XKDOUS safety eyes are on my Business Supplies list, confirmed on the live list page, with no personal note from me yet, so check the pack's size range before you buy for a specific project.

Is there a pattern book to pair with the yarn and eyes?

Yes, one already covered in a different post. Gifts for book lovers names Official Fourth Wing Crochet: Flight of Dragons, which includes Baby Andarna with a bonus Tairn, as a pattern book for a Fourth Wing fan who already has hooks and yarn. Pairing it with safety eyes and a skein of Bernat yarn turns one book into a complete gift.

How do you build a full gift from these pieces?

Match the yarn and eyes to the project the person already does. For amigurumi, pair the safety eyes with a smaller-weight yarn, not the bulky baby blanket yarn. For a blanket project, the Bernat baby blanket yarn alone is a complete gift on its own, since a blanket does not need eyes or a pattern book to start.

What if the person you are shopping for does not crochet yet?

Start smaller than a full kit. A single skein of yarn and the pattern book, without the safety eyes, is a lower-commitment gift for someone who has mentioned wanting to learn rather than someone already mid-project.

The bottom line on gifts for a crocheter

Match the gift to the project: yarn alone for a blanket maker, safety eyes and a smaller yarn for amigurumi, or the pattern book for someone just starting.
