The Random Things I Keep Buying for a Busy Household

← Back to Random Thoughts
Marie Harvey · November 15, 2026

Things I keep buying for a busy household are not always from one tidy list: a tumbler that holds enough coffee and water to skip a refill, a shacket I can throw on for drop-off, a desk set that keeps a cluttered spot looking put together, and the kitchen basics that never stop getting used. These five come from three different lists I keep on Benable, because no single list holds all of them yet.

What tumbler actually holds enough for a busy day?

One that holds coffee and water both. My Benable note on the Fall Tumbler, 40 oz in orange pumpkin and maple leaves, says it is big enough to hold coffee and water so you are not refilling every hour, and that it actually keeps drinks cold or hot in a house that never sits still. It is on my Cozy Fall Mom Life list.

What is an easy outfit layer for school drop-off?

A shacket, thrown over whatever you already have on. My note on the Beaully Women's Plaid Shacket Button Down says you throw it over a plain tee and leggings and look put together for school drop off with zero effort. It is on the same Cozy Fall Mom Life list as the tumbler above.

What keeps a cluttered desk spot from looking like a junk drawer?

A matching desk set. My note on the SKYDUE Purple Office Supplies Set, 11 pieces, calls it an 11-piece lavender desk set with a stapler, tape dispenser, scissors, and pencil holder, and says it is an easy way to make a workspace feel put together. It is on my Purple, Marble & Mom Life list, next to the family command center pieces I have already written about.

What kitchen basics are still worth buying even without a personal note?

Bakeware and everyday cooking utensils. My Moms Must Haves list links a Bakeware Sets category from Target and a Cooking Utensils category from Amazon. Neither one carries a specific personal note the way the tumbler or the shacket does, so I am naming them here as real, repeat-buy categories rather than reviewing one exact set.

Why do these five things share one post instead of their own?

Because none of them had enough company yet to fill a post on their own. A fall tumbler and a shacket are seasonal and personal. A desk set is an office pick. Bakeware and cooking utensils are kitchen basics. They do not belong to one tidy theme, but they are all real items I keep adding to a cart, which is the most honest way I can describe a busy household's actual buying list.

How do you decide which of these to buy first?

Buy for the thing you replace most often. If your tumbler never makes it through a morning, start there. If getting dressed for drop-off is the daily friction, start with the shacket. If your desk looks like a junk drawer every time you sit down, start with the desk set. Bakeware and utensils are the kind of thing you replace only once they wear out, so they are last on this list, not least important.

The bottom line on things I keep buying for a busy household

A busy household's real shopping list is rarely one category. I am a mom of seven in Wichita, Kansas, and I also run Marie Gems and Marie Glow Studio, so these five picks are genuinely what keeps showing up in my own cart across three different lists. My story and how the days actually work explain why. Prices on Benable and Amazon move around, so check each listing before you decide.
