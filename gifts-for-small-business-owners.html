What gift helps a business owner who works at a laptop all day?

Something for the eyes and something for the wrist. Every pick in this post is from my Pinterest Marketing Tools list on Benable, and my note on the Blue Light Glasses says design work means staring at a screen for hours planning pins, and my eyes notice the difference by the end of the day. My note on the Ergonomic Wrist Rest for Keyboard and Mouse calls it a small thing, and says your wrist will thank you after a full day of dragging and clicking in a design program. Both are small enough to wrap easily.

A Portable External SSD for client file backups. My note says cloud storage is great until the wifi is out or a client needs a file fast, so keep a backup drive with all your brand assets and finished pins on it. Check the listing for capacity and cable type before you order.

Which gifts help organize a busy desk?

Four small ones. The Desk Drawer Organizer keeps sticky notes, pens, SD cards, and business cards contained, and my note says it saves digging through a junk drawer before every client meeting. The Label Maker, in my note, gets grabbed almost every day for client folders, package labels, and cord organizing. The Sticky Notes for Content Planning are what I use to color-code pin ideas by client before anything goes into a real calendar. The Large Dry Erase Calendar for Wall, 38 by 50 inches, is big enough, my note says, to see the whole month of client content at a glance, and it erases clean when the schedule shifts.

What tech gifts make a desk tidier?

A charger and a phone stand. The 3-in-1 Wireless Charging Stand means, in my note, one less cord tangle on the desk and a phone that is always charged when a client calls or messages. The Phone Stand For Recording is for filming a quick demo or a Reel to show a client a pin idea in action, with no need to hold the phone the whole time.

Each bundle is small enough for a stocking, and the notes I wrote come from Pinterest strategy work, so they suit anyone who lives in a laptop and a calendar, not only Pinterest people.
