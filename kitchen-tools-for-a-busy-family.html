Kitchen Tools for a Busy Family: Slow Cooker to Skillet

← Back to Random Thoughts
Marie Harvey · October 13, 2026

The best kitchen tools for a busy family are the ones that cut hands-on time: a slow cooker, an immersion blender, a skillet that lives on the stove, and containers that make packing lunches quick. Those picks come from my Cozy Fall Mom Life and Moms Must Haves lists on Benable, plus a few Amazon items for cleanup.

Which kitchen tools for a busy family save the most time on a weeknight?

A slow cooker or a pressure cooker, depending on when your day falls apart. My Benable note on the Crock-Pot 6 Qt Cook & Carry Slow Cooker says to dump chili or soup ingredients in the morning and dinner is done by the time everyone is home from school. The Instant Pot Pressure Cooker on my Moms Must Haves list frees up a burner, and my note says it cuts way down on hands-on time for soups, rice, and shredded chicken. If your worst hour is the morning, the slow cooker fits. If it is five o'clock and nothing is started, the pressure cooker fits.

An immersion blender and one good pan. The Turelar 1100W 5-in-1 immersion blender blends butternut squash or tomato soup right in the pot, so there is no moving hot soup into a blender in batches. The Lodge Pre-Seasoned Cast Iron Skillet, 10.25 inch, is on my Cozy Fall Mom Life list for cornbread, skillet apples, or a one-pan dinner, and my note says it basically lives on the stove all fall.
