Sublimation Blanks for Holiday Gifts: Ornaments, Mugs, Totes

Sublimation blanks for holiday gifts are easiest to choose by asking what you plan to press: ornaments, mugs, keychains, phone cases, totes, or shirts. What can you sublimate for holiday gifts?

My Benable list covers six kinds of blanks besides tumblers. There are sublimation ornaments, sublimation mugs, Libbey cups, sublimation keychains, phone case blanks, and 100% polyester shirts. Of those, my note on the ornaments is the only one about the season. It says ornaments are a fun option if you want to branch out beyond shirts and mugs for gift season. Tote and makeup bag blanks are on my Amazon list. Tumblers have their own space on both lists, and I cover the press for them separately.

Which sublimation blank works as a low-cost add-on next to bigger pieces?

A small item next to a bigger one. The HTVRONT Sublimation Phone Case Blanks, a 6 pack, is on my Sublimation Must Haves list, and my note calls it a nice way to offer a smaller, lower-cost item alongside your bigger pieces. The MAFYE sublimation tote and makeup bag blanks come as a 30 pack and were listed at about $25 when I captured my list in September 2026, so check the current price. The ornaments and the keychains are on the same list, and my list has no note on the keychains.

Why do polyester blanks matter for sublimation?

Because fabric changes how well the color holds. My note on the 100% Polyester Shirts says 100% polyester holds color way better than cotton blends for sublimation printing, so check the fabric before you buy any shirt blank. For cotton shirts, I use DTF transfers at Marie Gems, and DTF vs. sublimation vs. HTV compares the three methods.

What do you need besides the blanks?

Four small supplies on my Benable list matter more than they look. My note on the Sublimation Tape says it keeps paper from shifting on the press, which it calls a must for crisp, lined-up transfers. The Heat Resistant Gloves protect your hands when you pull items off the press. The Magnetic Kitchen Timer sticks right to the press so you can keep track of pressing time without guessing. The SINGER Fabric Lint Roller is a quick way to remove lint and fuzz from blanks before pressing, so designs come out clean.

Do the ornaments and phone cases press the same way?

I cannot say, because my lists do not record pressing settings for any of these blanks. Each blank is made differently, so use the time and temperature that come with it, and press one test piece before you press a batch. The only pressing numbers I publish are for the DTF transfers I sell, which is a different method.

How early should you stock blanks for holiday orders?

Before the orders arrive, and in small packs first. My note on the Blank Coffee Mugs says plain white blanks are handy to keep on hand for custom or last-minute orders, and that is the best case for stocking early. Order the smallest pack of each blank you plan to sell, press a test piece, and only then order more. If you are making gifts for your own family and not for customers, the same order of operations applies with fewer blanks.

The bottom line on sublimation blanks for holiday gifts

Choose two or three blanks, add the small supplies, and test one piece before you commit.
