What does a sublimation convection oven do that a heat press does not?

It bakes a design instead of pressing it, which matters for a curved or oddly shaped blank. The PYD Life sublimation convection oven, 25L is on my Sublimation Starter Kit list. I have not written a personal note on it, so it is named here as a real item on my list, not a reviewed one. Is sublimation sticker paper different from regular sublimation paper?

Yes, it is a separate product made for a sticky-back finish rather than a transfer onto a blank. The HTVRONT sublimation sticker paper, 20 sheets is on the same list, with no written note from me, so check the listing for which printers it is actually compatible with before you buy a pack.

Why buy tumbler blanks in bulk instead of one at a time?

Because a bulk pack is cheaper per tumbler and keeps you from running out mid-batch. A 24-pack of 30 oz blanks covers more orders between restocks than buying 4 at a time, which matters once pressing becomes a regular part of running a shop instead of a one-off project.

Frequently asked questions

Is a sublimation oven the same as a heat press?

No. A heat press presses flat against a blank, while a convection oven like the PYD Life 25L bakes a design, which works better for curved or odd-shaped blanks that a press cannot flatten against evenly.

Why buy tumbler blanks in a bulk pack?

A bulk pack, like the Muwnrol 30 oz 24-pack, is cheaper per tumbler and keeps you from running out mid-batch once pressing is a regular part of running a shop, not just a one-off project.
