Check the listing for size, power, and delivery. Look at the dimensions against the table or shelf it will sit on. Then check the delivery estimate against the date you need it. Who is a Peanuts gift right for?

A Peanuts gift is right for someone who already loves Snoopy and Woodstock. A collectible is a gift for a fan, not a gamble on someone who has never mentioned the comic strip.

How do you choose between a tabletop tree and a wall piece?

Choose by where the gift will live. A tabletop tree is only up for the holidays and needs a table, while a wall clock is a piece for a wall. Pick the one that fits the room you know the person has.
