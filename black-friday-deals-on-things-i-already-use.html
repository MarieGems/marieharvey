Black Friday Deals on the Real Equipment I Already Use

← Back to Random Thoughts
Marie Harvey · November 22, 2026

Black Friday deals are worth watching this year for the equipment I already use and recommend, not for anything new. I have not seen this year's actual Black Friday prices yet, so this is a list of what to watch, not a list of confirmed discounts. Four categories from my own Business Owner Favorites posts are the ones worth checking when the sales start: a heat press, a DTF printer, a thermal label printer, and a standing desk.

Why watch for sales on a heat press specifically?

Because it is one of the bigger single purchases on any DTF or sublimation supply list. Which heat press fits DTF transfers from 4 to 12 inches already compares platen sizes and prices as of when it was written. If you have been putting off a press because of the price, Black Friday and Cyber Monday are the days actual retailers run sales on equipment like this, more often than on smaller accessories.

Is a DTF printer worth waiting for a sale on?

If you were already planning to buy one, yes, because it is the single biggest cost on a DTF business starter kit. DTF printing supplies compares a printer with an oven against ink and powder. A printer is not an impulse buy at any price, so there is little downside to waiting a few extra weeks to see where Black Friday pricing lands.

What about a thermal label printer?

It is a smaller purchase, but one that still shows up on sale lists most years. A thermal label printer setup for a small product shop covers the printer, holder, and sticker side of shipping. If you are still hand-writing labels, this is a lower-risk category to watch, since the whole setup costs less than a press or a printer either way.

Is a standing desk really a Black Friday category?

Furniture categories run sales too, not just electronics. My home office must-haves names the L-shaped electric standing desk I actually use. Furniture this size is worth comparing across a few retailers during a sale window rather than buying the first listing you see, since shipping costs on a piece this large can eat into any discount.

What should you check before buying any of these on sale?

The real price history, not just the sale badge. A listing marked down from an inflated "original" price is not actually a deal, so check a price tracking tool, or at least your own memory of what the item has cost before, if you have looked at it more than once. A real discount is a lower number than what the item has actually sold for recently, not just what the tag claims.

Should you wait for Black Friday on something you need right now?

No. If a heat press or a printer is actually blocking your business this week, buy it this week. A possible discount a few days out is not worth stalling real orders over, and nothing about this list is a promise that this year's prices will actually drop.

The bottom line on Black Friday deals

Watch the categories, not a specific product, and compare any sale price against what the item has actually cost before you decide it is a deal. I run Marie Gems out of Wichita, Kansas, and the equipment on these lists is the same equipment I press and ship orders on myself. My Brands page shows what I have built with it. Prices move around on every platform, so check each listing before you decide.
