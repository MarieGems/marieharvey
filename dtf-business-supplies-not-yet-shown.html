DTF Business Supplies: 7 Real Picks I Have Not Shown

← Back to Random Thoughts
Marie Harvey · November 16, 2026

DTF business supplies worth adding to your own setup don't stop at the printer and the press. On September 21, 2026, a read-through of my live Business Supplies list on Amazon found seven real items that no Random Thoughts post has named yet: a blank hoodie multipack, DTF cup wrap stickers, an HTV cooling block, a mini heat press, a roll of thermal labels, aluminum bookmark blanks, and a crystal paperweight for the desk. I run Marie Gems, a DTF print shop, and these are the supplies still sitting on my own list waiting for their turn.

What blank apparel is on my Business Supplies list?

A fleece hoodie multipack. The Gildan G18500 fleece hoodie multipack is on my list as a blank, meaning it is the garment itself before any design goes on it. A hoodie is heavier and bulkier to press than a T-shirt, so check your press plate size against the hoodie's actual panel before you commit to a design placement.

Is a DTF cup wrap sticker the same as a DTF transfer?

No, it is a different product shape. The CHNVICK UV DTF cup wrap sticker is sized to wrap around a tumbler or cup rather than press onto fabric. UV DTF is a separate process from the DTF transfers I sell at Marie Gems, which press onto apparel at 320°F for 15 seconds with a cold peel. A cup wrap sticker does not go through a heat press the same way, so my own pressing numbers do not apply to it.

What keeps an HTV cooling block from making it onto most supply lists?

It solves a narrow problem: cooling a transfer fast between presses. The HAWERSI HTV cooling block is built to bring a just-pressed design down to a safe handling temperature quickly, which matters more once you are pressing back to back instead of one order at a time. It is not something a beginner needs on day one, but it earns its spot once volume picks up.

Is a mini heat press a replacement for a full-size press?

No, only an addition for a specific job. The HTVRONT mini heat press is a smaller unit than the presses I already compare in which heat press fits DTF transfers from 4 to 12 inches, and a smaller platen fits a smaller design, like a sleeve or a pocket, rather than a full front print. Think of it as a second station, not your main press.

Do you need Phomemo thermal labels if you already have a label printer?

Yes, since a printer does not come with an unlimited label supply built in. Phomemo 4x6 thermal labels are a roll of labels themselves, separate from the printer and holder I cover in a thermal label printer setup for a small product shop. Check the roll's core size and label material against whatever printer you run, since thermal label rolls are not universal across every printer brand.

What are the aluminum sublimation bookmark blanks for?

They are a blank, not a finished product. The GEKIXUTP aluminum sublimation bookmark blanks are made for sublimation printing, a different method from the DTF transfers I press at Marie Gems, and they give you a small, low-cost item to test a new design on before committing it to a bigger blank.

Is a desk paperweight really a business supply?

A small one, but a real one. The Adwikoso purple crystal diamond paperweight sits on the same Business Supplies list as the equipment above, and purple is the color that runs through my own sites. I have not written a personal note on it, so it is named here as a real item on my list rather than reviewed.

The bottom line on these DTF business supplies

Start with whichever item solves the problem you actually have this week: a blank hoodie for a new product line, a cooling block once volume picks up, or a mini heat press for small placements the big platen cannot reach.
