Living Room and Dining Room Finds for a Big Family

← Back to Random Thoughts
Marie Harvey · November 8, 2026

Living room and dining room finds for a big family need to do one job well: hold more people than a small household would ever need to seat or feed. The picks below come from my HOME MUST HAVES list on Amazon and my Purple, Marble & Mom Life list on Benable, for the rooms where everyone ends up at once.

What seating actually works for a big family's living room?

Something oversized that can also sleep a guest. The Belffin oversized modular velvet sectional sleeper is on my HOME MUST HAVES list. I have not written a note on it, so check the listing for the actual sleeper dimensions and the modular piece count before you measure your room against it.

What dining table fits a big family?

One that can grow. The STHOUYN extendable white dining table is on the same list. An extendable table means the everyday footprint can stay smaller while still having a leaf or extension ready for the nights the table actually needs to seat everyone, including anyone who stays for dinner.

What decorative pillows are on my list for a living room?

Two lines of lumbar pillows with words on them. The "Sweet Dreams / Good Night" lumbar pillowcases and the "Love Yourself / Blessed" lumbar pillow covers are both on my HOME MUST HAVES list. Neither carries a note from me, so they are named here as real items, not personally reviewed pieces. Check the listing for the insert size each one is made for.

Is there a good DIY sign project for a family room?

Yes, a blank you finish yourself. The Unfinished Wood Sign Blank Rectangle, a 6-pack, is on my Purple, Marble & Mom Life list on Benable, and my note calls it a good base for a custom family name sign with vinyl lettering. With seven kids in the house, a sign with the family's own name on it is the kind of project that uses more than one of the six blanks.

How do you furnish a living and dining room for a big family without overspending?

Buy the pieces that do double duty first. A sleeper sofa is seating every day and a guest bed on the nights you need one, and an extendable table is a smaller table on a normal night and a full table when it counts. The decorative pillows and the DIY sign are the cheap finishing touches you add once the big pieces are already in place.

Why pull from two different lists for one room?

Because no single list covers both furniture and small decor. My HOME MUST HAVES list on Amazon is where the big furniture pieces live, and my Purple, Marble & Mom Life list on Benable is where the smaller, more personal pieces like a custom family sign show up. Between the two, a room gets both its big pieces and its finishing touches.

The bottom line on living room and dining room finds

Start with the sectional sleeper or the extendable table, whichever room needs the bigger fix, then add the pillows and a DIY sign once the furniture is settled.
