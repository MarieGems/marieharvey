Small Home Upgrades I Keep Adding From My Amazon List

← Back to Random Thoughts
Marie Harvey · November 3, 2026

Small home upgrades for a busy mom don't need a renovation, just the right item in the right spot: a bath rug that actually grips, a toothbrush holder that keeps the counter clear, and a corner of the house set up for a quick workout instead of a gym membership. These six picks are all on my HOME MUST HAVES list on Amazon, which has 31 items in total.

What bathroom upgrades are on my list?

Two small ones: a rug and a toothbrush holder. The OLANLY black chenille bath rug and the Wbbog stainless toothbrush holder are both on my HOME MUST HAVES list. I have not written a note on either one, so check the listing for size and material before you order, the same as any item I have not personally reviewed in writing.

Why do small items like a rug or a holder count as "upgrades"?

Because the upgrade is what the item replaces, not its price tag. A bath rug that actually grips replaces one that slides every time, and a stainless toothbrush holder replaces a counter where seven kids' toothbrushes just end up loose. The upgrade is solving the daily annoyance, not spending more money.

Is a shower conditioner really worth calling a home upgrade?

Only in the sense that it is one less thing to run out of mid shower. The Dove coconut and hydration conditioner, 28 oz is on my list because the large size means fewer trips to restock it, not because of anything more dramatic. I have no written note on it, so this is a practical pick, not a review.

Can you fit home workout equipment into a busy house?

If the equipment folds or rolls away, yes. The JANUA pilates reformer board, the Yagud incline walking pad, and the MERACH ab machine are all on my list, and all three are the kind of equipment made to tuck against a wall or under a bed when it is not in use. I have not written a note on any of the three, so check the listing for the folded dimensions before you count on it fitting your space.

What should you check before buying home exercise equipment?

The folded size and the floor space it needs mid-workout, not just the price. A reformer board, a walking pad, and an ab machine all look compact in a product photo, but each one needs real floor space while you are using it, even if it folds flat afterward. Measure the space you actually have before you add any of the three to a cart.

Which of these upgrades would you start with?

Start with whichever room causes you the most daily friction. If it is the bathroom counter, start with the rug and the holder. If it is finding time to move between kid pickups, start with whichever piece of equipment folds smallest for your space.

Why only six items from a 31-item list?

Because the rest already have a home in other posts. My HOME MUST HAVES list also covers the purple rug and pillow covers in purple home decor finds and the key and mail holder and storage benches in family command center ideas, so this post sticks to the six that have not been covered yet.

The bottom line on small home upgrades

None of these need a renovation or a big budget, just a decision about which daily friction point to fix first. I am a mom of seven in Wichita, Kansas, and I also run Marie Gems and Marie Glow Studio, so small, low-effort upgrades are the kind that actually stick in my house. Self care for busy moms and family command center ideas cover other corners of the house from different lists. Prices on Amazon move around, so check each listing before you decide.
