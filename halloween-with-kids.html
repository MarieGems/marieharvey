Halloween With Kids: Trunk or Treat, Costumes, Pumpkins

← Back to Random Thoughts
Marie Harvey · October 19, 2026

Halloween with kids goes best when the three big jobs are handled early: the costume, the car for trunk or treat, and a pumpkin night at home. My Moms Must Haves list on Benable has a trunk or treat car kit and two costumes, my Cozy Fall Mom Life list has a carving kit, a craft set, and bonfire gear, and my Peanuts Collectibles list on Amazon has two Great Pumpkin sculptures for the shelf.

What should you sort out first for Halloween with kids?

Start with the costume and the trunk or treat kit, because both have to arrive and fit before the night. Pumpkin carving, crafts, and warm layers can wait until the week of. I am a mom of seven in Wichita, Kansas, so the picks below lean toward things that keep a crowd busy without much setup. Every item is on one of my lists, and where my Benable note says something about it, I tell you what the note says.
