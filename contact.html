Frequently asked questions

Who is Marie Harvey?

Marie Harvey is a dual-threat business owner and mom of seven based in Wichita, Kansas. She runs two businesses solo — Marie Gems, a DTF transfer and custom product shop, and Lavender Pin Co, a Pinterest strategy, email marketing, and Shopify web design brand.

What is Marie Gems?

Marie Gems is a DTF transfer and custom product shop, three years in the making. It sells across Shopify, Vinted, Whatnot, Etsy, and direct sales.

What is Lavender Pin Co?

Lavender Pin Co is Marie Harvey's Pinterest strategy, email marketing, and Shopify web design brand for product-based businesses that want to get found and look like themselves once they do.

Does Marie Harvey run her businesses alone?

Yes. Marie runs both Marie Gems and Lavender Pin Co herself — no team, no outsourcing — while raising seven kids.

How can I work with Marie Harvey?

For Pinterest strategy, email marketing, or Shopify web design help, reach out through Lavender Pin Co. For DTF transfers and custom products, shop Marie Gems. For anything else, use the contact form on this page.

Wichita, Kansas.
