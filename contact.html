Frequently asked questions

Who is Marie Harvey?

Marie Harvey is a dual-threat business owner and mom of seven based in Wichita, Kansas. She runs two businesses solo — Marie Gems, a DTF transfer and custom product shop, and Marie Glow Studio, a web design, Pinterest marketing, and SEO studio.

What is Marie Gems?

Marie Gems is a DTF transfer and custom product shop, three years in the making. It sells across Shopify, Vinted, Whatnot, Etsy, and direct sales.

What is Marie Glow Studio?

Marie Glow Studio is Marie Harvey's digital studio for web design and development, Pinterest marketing, and SEO and digital strategy, for product and service small businesses that want to get found and look like themselves once they do.

Does Marie Harvey run her businesses alone?

Yes. Marie runs both Marie Gems and Marie Glow Studio herself — no team, no outsourcing — while raising seven kids.

How can I work with Marie Harvey?

For web design, Pinterest marketing, or SEO help, reach out through Marie Glow Studio. For DTF transfers and custom products, shop Marie Gems. For anything else, use the contact form on this page.

Where is Marie Harvey located?

Wichita, Kansas.
