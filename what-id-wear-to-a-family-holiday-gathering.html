What I'd Wear to a Family Holiday Gathering This Year

← Back to Random Thoughts
Marie Harvey · November 26, 2026

What I'd wear to a family holiday gathering this year comes down to three real pieces on my Amazon list: a dressy casual jumpsuit, a crop top and wide-leg pants set, and a cocktail dress with a floral tulle skirt. All three are real items on my list that no other post has named yet, and none of them need anything fancier than what is already in a normal closet to go with them.

What makes a jumpsuit a good pick for a family gathering?

One piece, dressed up just enough without actually being formal. The PRETTYGARDEN dressy casual jumpsuit is on my list with no written note from me, so check the listing for the actual fit and length before you order, since a jumpsuit runs differently by brand more than most other pieces.

Is a crop top and wide-leg pants set too casual for a holiday dinner?

Not if the pants are the dressier kind of wide-leg, which is the whole point of pairing them together instead of wearing either piece alone. The KEYUFANG 2-piece crop top and wide-leg pants set is on the same list, and a matching two-piece set reads more put-together than separates picked from two different places in a closet.

What is the dressiest option on this list?

A cocktail dress with a floral tulle skirt. The Gleeneat halter floral tulle cocktail dress is the one piece on this list built for an actual party rather than a casual family dinner, so save it for the gathering with more going on than a plated meal at someone's house.

How do you choose between these three for a specific event?

Match the dress code of the room, not just the holiday on the calendar. A casual family dinner suits the jumpsuit or the two-piece set. A party with extended family, a work holiday event, or anything with photos planned suits the cocktail dress. The event matters more than the date. For an everyday layer instead of a holiday outfit, the shacket I wear for school drop-off is covered in the random things I keep buying for a busy household.

Are there other pieces on this same list?

Yes, two more, but they are not a good fit for this time of year. An empire-waist spring dress and a mini summer sundress are also on my list, both built for warmer weather. I am holding those two for a spring post instead of forcing them into a late-November gift guide where they do not actually fit.

What should you check before ordering any of these three?

The actual size chart on the listing, not just the size you normally wear. None of these three carries a written note from me on fit, so treat the listing's own measurements as the real source, especially on a jumpsuit or a fitted cocktail dress where a size can run differently than a basic top would.

The bottom line on dressing for a family holiday gathering

Pick the piece that matches the room, not the fanciest option on the list.
