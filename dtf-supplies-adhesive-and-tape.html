DTF Supplies I Have Not Covered Yet: Adhesive and Tape

← Back to Random Thoughts
Marie Harvey · November 28, 2026

DTF hot melt adhesive powder and heat press tape are the two supplies on my DTF Business Starter Kit list that an earlier post about the printer and the press has not named yet. That list on Benable has 16 items, and these two protect your prints and your press from small mistakes that actually ruin a batch.

What does hot melt adhesive powder actually do?

It is what makes the print stick to the fabric and survive the wash, not an optional add-on. My Benable note on DTF Hot Melt Adhesive Powder White, 500g, says it is what makes the print stick and survive the wash, and that you press it in after the film cools, not while it is still hot. It is on my DTF Business Starter Kit list.

What does heat press tape actually prevent?

Film shifting mid-press, which ruins a transfer in a way that is hard to catch until the print is already peeled. My note on Heat Press Tape says it keeps your film from shifting mid-press, and calls it a small thing that saves you a lot of ruined transfers. It is the cheapest item of the two and the one most worth keeping stocked.

Why does the adhesive powder need to go on after the film cools?

Because the ink is still wet and warm right off the printer, and pressing powder into warm, wet ink before it sets is how the powder ends up uneven instead of bonded where it needs to be. Cooling the film first is the step that makes the rest of the process predictable.

Which of these two should you buy first?

The adhesive powder, since nothing presses onto fabric without it. Heat press tape matters once you are pressing regularly and film shifting has already cost you a transfer, but a shop cannot run at all without the powder.

The bottom line on these two DTF supplies

Stock the adhesive powder before you ever press a transfer, and add the heat press tape once you are pressing often enough that a shifted film actually costs you something.
