Nursery and Baby Gear: A Dresser, Crib, and Car Seats

← Back to Random Thoughts
Marie Harvey · November 19, 2026

Nursery and baby gear from a dresser to a car seat is the half of my baby lists that has not made it into a post yet. My earlier post on baby and toddler essentials already covers bottle care, pumping, and potty training. This one covers the bigger gear: where the clothes go, what carries baby in the car, and a pregnancy pillow for the months before any of it matters.

What dresser actually fits a nursery?

One with enough drawers to sort by size, not just by type. The Jocoevol 6 drawer dresser is on my Business Supplies list, confirmed on the live list page in 2026, and six drawers is enough to separate onesies, pajamas, and the next size up before baby actually grows into it. Check the listing for the real drawer depth before you count on it holding more than folded basics.

Is a pregnancy pillow worth buying before baby arrives?

If sleep is already hard, yes. The Momcozy U-shaped pregnancy pillow is on my Baby and Mom Must Haves list, and a U-shape supports both sides of the body at once instead of needing a pillow swapped from side to side through the night. It is a pregnancy item, not a baby item, so it earns its spot on this list before the nursery gear does.

What should you check before buying a car seat?

The weight and height limits, not just the age range printed on the box. Toddler Car Seats are on my Moms Must Haves list as a category, and every seat in that category has its own real limits. I have not written a personal note ranking one car seat over another, so treat this as a category to compare rather than a single recommendation, and check your vehicle's actual back seat before you commit to one.

What about a crib, a high chair, and a playard?

Three more categories on the same list, each with its own real measurements to check. Cribs, Highchairs, and Playards in Baby Activities & Gear are all on my Moms Must Haves list. A crib's mattress size, a high chair's tray clearance at your table, and a playard's folded size for storage are the three numbers worth checking before any of the three ships.

Is cloth diapering realistic for a bigger family?

It depends on your laundry capacity more than your patience. Cloth Diapers are on the same Moms Must Haves list, and the honest tradeoff is more loads of laundry for less ongoing cost per diaper. With seven kids in my own house, laundry volume is already a factor in nearly every decision like this one.

Where do the smaller baby items fit instead?

In the post that already covers them. Bottle warmers, pumping gear, teethers, and the potty training chart are all in baby and toddler essentials, which pulls from the same two lists this post does. This post sticks to the bigger gear and the dresser that holds it.

The bottom line on nursery and baby gear

Start with whichever piece has a hard deadline, the car seat before a hospital discharge, the crib before baby outgrows a bassinet, then add the dresser and the smaller gear as the nursery comes together.
