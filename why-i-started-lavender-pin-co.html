Why I Started Lavender Pin Co After Building Marie Gems

← Back to Random Thoughts
Marie Harvey · September 2, 2026

Building Marie Gems taught me how to build a website. It also taught me something I wasn't expecting: building a website and getting anyone to actually find it are two completely different skills. You can do the first one perfectly and still end up with a beautiful shop that nobody sees.

Where the gap showed up

I came into this with a real web design background — an associate's degree, then a bachelor's in web design and development. So the building part wasn't the problem. I knew how to make something functional and good-looking. What I didn't fully understand until I was running my own shop was that "built" and "found" are two separate jobs, and nobody had really taught me the second one.

What that gap pushed me toward

Once I saw that gap clearly, I couldn't unsee it. It pushed me hard into learning SEO, marketing, and specifically Pinterest — because Pinterest turned out to be one of the most effective ways to get real, sustained traffic to a small product-based shop without needing a huge ad budget. I wasn't studying it for fun. I was studying it because Marie Gems needed it to actually work.

Why that lesson became a second business

Here's the part that surprised me: once I'd solved that problem for my own shop, I realized how many other small business owners were sitting exactly where I'd been — a website they'd built or paid for, and no real plan for getting anyone to it. That's not a rare problem. It's close to universal for anyone running a product-based business without a marketing background.

So Lavender Pin Co became the place where that lesson turns into something useful for other people. Pinterest strategy and email marketing, specifically for product-based businesses that are exactly where Marie Gems used to be — built, but not found yet.

What that actually looks like

It's not generic marketing advice. It's the specific thing I had to learn the hard way: how to take a website that already exists and actually get people to it, using the same tools and strategy that turned Pinterest into a real traffic source for my own shop. If that's the gap you're sitting in right now — a site that's built but quiet — that's exactly what Lavender Pin Co exists to close.
