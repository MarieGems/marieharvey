Stems, garland, and one grab-and-go sign. The Fall Pumpkin Wall Signs are an Amazon's Choice pick, and my note calls the three-tier hanging sign a nice grab-and-go piece for a mantle or entry wall when you do not have time to style something from scratch.

The 14 inch black fall lanterns. My note says they come pre-decorated with faux pumpkins and leaves, so they work as an instant centerpiece for a porch or entryway table with no extra styling needed.

What is a cheap way to fill a bowl or vase for fall?

Filler pieces, bought in bulk. My note on the Syhood 200 piece artificial acorns and pine cones says 200 pieces goes a long way as a bowl or vase filler, and that mixing them with mini pumpkins gives a fuller, more textured look on a tabletop or mantle. On my FALL Decor list on Amazon, the Moucuny wooden maple leaf blocks, a 4 pack, are a smaller filler piece in the same spirit, though I have not written a note on that one.

How do you put a mantle together from these pieces in order?

Start with the stems or the garland as the base layer, add the wall sign or lanterns as the anchor piece, then scatter the pumpkins and filler pieces last. That is the order my own notes suggest, a base that looks real, one grab-and-go anchor, then the small stuff that fills gaps.

The bottom line on fall mantle and tabletop decor

Start with whichever surface looks the most bare, the mantle or the table, and build it in layers instead of buying everything at once.
