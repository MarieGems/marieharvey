Gifts for Book Lovers: Fourth Wing Finds and Nook Ideas

With Christmas ahead, the best gifts for book lovers go around the reading: a series set to keep on the shelf, a tumbler for the cup beside it, and a corner to read in. My Amazon links include six Fourth Wing items, a Cozy Purple Reading Nook list of 11 items, and a Kindle on my Lavender Gift Set list. The picks below are sorted by the kind of reader you are shopping for.

What is a good gift for a Fourth Wing fan?

Start with books, then add one piece of merchandise. The Fourth Wing series 3-book set is on my list, and so is Threshing Day, from the Wing and Claw Collection of The Empyrean, for the reader who already owns the main books. Check each listing to confirm the edition and format before you order.

Which Fourth Wing gifts are not books?

Three of them: a tumbler, keychains, and shelf decor. The Fufendio Fourth Wing 40 oz tumbler with handle and straw suits someone who carries a drink everywhere. The Fourth Wing dragon keychains, 2 inch are the smallest gift of the group, which makes them an easy stocking add-on. The TwoDays dragon wing bookshelf display decor is for someone with a shelf to fill. Check each listing for size and what is included.

Is there a Fourth Wing gift for someone who crochets?

Official Fourth Wing Crochet: Flight of Dragons is on my list, and its listing title names Baby Andarna with a bonus Tairn. It is a crochet book, so it fits a reader who already has hooks and yarn. My Business Supplies list has the Katech 100% cotton yarn, 12 colors, which would pair with it. Check the crochet book's listing for skill level and which yarn weight it calls for before you buy the two together.

What gifts help someone set up a reading nook?

Pick the piece that fits the room, because the big ones need floor space. My Cozy Purple Reading Nook list has three oversized seats: the GRUSIGN 66 inch oversized chaise lounge, which is also listed as a cloud reading chair, the VEPXXP 65.4 inch oversized boneless chaise lounge, and the feliz home boneless chaise with an ottoman. For storage, it has the RORKIM purple acrylic floating shelves, 15 inch, 4 pack, the SHEEPAM 10-tier tree bookshelf, and the AOPNOMU kids bookcase with 3 fabric drawers. Measure the space and check the delivery date before you order a large piece as a gift.

How do you choose between the three chaise options?

Compare length, seating style, and what is included. The GRUSIGN is listed at 66 inches, the VEPXXP at 65.4 inches, and the feliz home model comes with an ottoman, which the other two do not mention in their listing titles. If the reader wants to stretch out with their feet up, the ottoman may decide it. If floor space is tight, measure first, because all three are oversized. Read the listings for fill, cover material, and cleaning instructions before you pick.

What small gifts keep track of a year of reading?

A sign and a counter. The SRADMO "I'm Reading" floral wood box sign announces that someone is reading, and the DAUCHVNY "Books Read This Year" counter keeps a running tally. My list also has a second listing of the same counter, so compare the two before you choose.

What other gifts suit a reader?

A device, or something custom. My Lavender Gift Set list on Amazon has 5 items, including the Kindle Paperwhite Signature Edition, 32GB, for a reader who wants a light way to carry a library. If you want something custom and bookish, Marie Gems, my DTF print shop, has book club among its catalog themes.

The bottom line on gifts for book lovers

Match the gift to how the person reads. A series fan gets the set and a tumbler, someone with a stack of books waiting gets a chair and a shelf, and someone who likes to count gets the counter.
