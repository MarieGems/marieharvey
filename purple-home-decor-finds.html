Purple home decor looks intentional when it has an anchor color around it, and my lists build that with white marble and a little gold. The purple home decor finds below come from my Purple, Marble & Mom Life list and my Household Decor list on Benable, plus a rug and pillow covers from my HOME MUST HAVES list on Amazon.

How do you make purple decor look intentional instead of random?

Choose one purple and let everything else be quiet around it. White marble and a touch of gold are the two neutrals my lists keep returning to, and the plain surfaces let a purple piece stand out. Purple is also the main color across my own sites, so it is the direction my home lists lean. My Purple, Marble & Mom Life list has 17 items and describes itself as purple decor, marble accents, and practical finds for moms juggling a big family.

What purple rug is on my Amazon list?
